Leadership & Background

Bridging Ecological Science, Satellite Analytics & Geospatial Engineering

GeoPhoenix was founded by Teshan Ishara to address a persistent gap in spatial consulting: bridging deep ecological science with modern, reproducible geospatial computing. Holding a Bachelor of Science (Honors) in Fisheries and Marine Sciences from the University of Ruhuna, Sri Lanka, Teshan approaches spatial challenges with both biological domain understanding and technical computational rigor.

As a collaborative Research Assistant for projects with the University of Southampton, UK (Faculty of Engineering and Physical Sciences), Teshan conducts high-level spatial modeling and academic data analysis under the supervision of PhD researchers. Over the past 6+ years, he has successfully delivered more than 150 international projects for environmental consultancies, engineering teams, government bodies, and universities spanning the USA, Canada, UK, Europe, and Australia.

Engineering Arsenal

Technical Expertise & Core Toolset

Production-grade technologies utilized across our consulting practice.

Desktop GIS Software

Advanced mastery of QGIS 3.x (custom models, PyQGIS, atlas generation) and ArcGIS Pro (geodatabase design, spatial analyst, 3D analyst, spatial statistics).

Python Geospatial Stack

Automated vector/raster pipelines built with GeoPandas, Rasterio, Shapely, PyProj, GDAL/OGR, Fiona, Xarray, and Matplotlib/Cartopy.

Cloud Earth Observation

Large-scale satellite analytics utilizing the Google Earth Engine (GEE) JavaScript and Python APIs, processing petabyte-scale imagery collections.

Statistical Modeling & R

Quantitative statistical modeling using R (sf, spdep, ggplot2) and SPSS for academic hypothesis testing, multivariate analysis, and spatial econometrics.
