Company Profile

Geospatial Intelligence. Research. Real-World Solutions.

GeoPhoenix is an international geospatial solutions consultancy serving environmental consultancies, civil engineering firms, municipal authorities, universities, and commercial organizations. We bridge ecological science, remote sensing physics, and modern Python geoprocessing to solve complex spatial problems.

Led by Founder and Lead GIS Specialist Teshan Ishara—a graduate with Honors in Fisheries & Marine Sciences and collaborative Research Assistant for projects with the University of Southampton, UK—GeoPhoenix provides defensible spatial evidence adhering to peer-reviewed scientific and international cartographic standards.

Our Operating Principles

  • Scientific Rigor: Geodetic projections, calibrated surface reflectance, and confusion matrix validation on all models.
  • Full Data Ownership: Complete transfer of raw vector layers, GeoPackages, GeoTIFFs, and Python codebase.
  • Global Delivery Standards: Compliant with ISO 19115 and OGC spatial metadata guidelines.
  • Guaranteed Confidentiality: Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) protection for client spatial assets.
