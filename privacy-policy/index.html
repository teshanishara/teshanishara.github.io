Privacy Policy & Client Data Confidentiality

GeoPhoenix is committed to safeguarding client privacy, proprietary spatial boundaries, and project research data. This document outlines our data collection, handling, non-disclosure protocols, and compliance with global privacy standards.

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure (NDA)

In the geospatial consulting domain, client data frequently includes sensitive intellectual property: proprietary cadastral parcel boundaries, confidential mineral exploration coordinates, unpublished research survey points, infrastructure asset inventories, and private land tenure records.

Our Non-Disclosure Guarantee: All spatial layers, attribute databases, raw coordinates, raster drone datasets, and working deliverables entrusted to GeoPhoenix are held in strict confidence. GeoPhoenix does not share, resell, publish, or use client-provided spatial data for any purpose other than fulfilling the contracted consulting scope. We routinely execute bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to data transfer upon client request.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following contexts:

  • Inquiry & RFQ Form Data: Full name, professional organization, email address, service requirements, geographic scope, and project budget communicated via our inquiry forms or direct email.
  • Spatial Datasets: Vector shapefiles, GeoPackages, CAD files, CSV coordinates, and raster files transferred by clients for analytical modeling.
  • Technical Website Telemetry: Standard web visit telemetry via Google Analytics 4 (GA4), including anonymized IP addresses, browser types, referring pages, device operating systems, and session interaction metrics.

3. How We Use Information

Collected information is utilized strictly to:

  • Evaluate project technical feasibility and prepare formal consulting proposals.
  • Execute mathematical geoprocessing, satellite data extraction, and cartographic deliverables.
  • Communicate milestone progress and deliver project packages.
  • Analyze website aggregate traffic performance to improve documentation clarity and site navigation.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

Client spatial datasets are processed in secure, encrypted local development environments with restricted access. Following project completion, formal sign-off, and delivery of final assets:

  • Active working project files are archived in secure, offline encrypted backups for a standard warranty window of 90 days to facilitate client follow-ups or revision requests.
  • Upon client request or after the retention period, all client source data is permanently and irreversibly purged from our storage drives.

5. Cookies & Analytics

GeoPhoenix uses standard cookies and Google Analytics 4 measurement protocols to evaluate aggregate visitor engagement (e.g. tracking page views, conversion event clicks on contact methods, and form initiation). No personally identifiable spatial survey data is transmitted to analytics providers. Visitors may configure their browsers to decline cookies without impairing their ability to view our website.

6. Third-Party Sub-Processors

GeoPhoenix utilizes reputable third-party infrastructure providers that maintain high security and privacy standards:

  • GitHub Pages: Static website hosting provider (Microsoft Corporation).
  • Web3Forms: Encrypted form transmission provider that relays inquiry emails directly to our secure business mailbox without persisting sensitive databases.
  • Fiverr International: Optional escrow contracting platform for clients who elect to engage services via Fiverr's freelance marketplace.

7. GDPR & International Privacy Rights

In accordance with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and international privacy frameworks, clients and website visitors possess the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about them.
  • Request the immediate correction or deletion of their personal records and submitted files.
  • Withdraw consent for communications at any time.
